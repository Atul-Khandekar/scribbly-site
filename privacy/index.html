Privacy Policy for Scribbly

Last updated: March 3, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Scribbly app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created as a free, ad-supported service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application does not require you to create an account or provide any identifying personal information (such as your name, email, or phone number). The Application collects the following information:

  • Usernames: When joining a multiplayer room, you select a temporary username. This is only stored temporarily in memory on our game server for the duration of the match and is not saved permanently.
  • Game Data: Your drawings and chat guesses are transmitted to other players in your room in real-time. This data is discarded when the room closes.

Third-Party Services and Advertising

The Application uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, primarily for serving advertisements. The Application uses Google AdMob to display ads.

Link to the privacy policy of third-party service providers used by the Application:

  • Google Play Services
  • AdMob

These third-party ad networks may use Advertising IDs (such as the Android Advertising ID) and cookies/device identifiers to serve personalized ads based on your interests and past usage.

Data Retention and Security

We do not store or retain any personal data on our servers. All game-state data (drawings, player scores, temporary usernames) exists only in active memory and is permanently destroyed the moment a game session concludes. We rely on the security protocols of our hosting platforms to protect data in transit.

Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at atul@playscribbly.com.
