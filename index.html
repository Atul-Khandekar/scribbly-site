Three ways to play

Friends, strangers or the Scribbly Bot

However you like to play, a game is only a few taps away.

Up to 8 players

Private rooms

Create a room and share its code with up to 7 friends. Everyone joins from their own phone, wherever they are.

2, 3 or 4 players

Pick a match size and Scribbly finds opponents for you. The game starts as soon as the room fills.

No signal? Play a full game against the Scribbly Bot, completely offline.

How a game works

Four steps, three rounds, one champion

  1. 1

    Pick a word

    Each turn, the artist chooses one of three fresh words. No word comes up twice in the same game.

  2. 2

    Draw it

    You get 80 seconds, 12 colours, three brush sizes, an eraser and undo. Everyone watches your drawing appear live.

  3. 3

    Guess fast

    Type your guesses as the picture takes shape. Letters appear as the clock runs down, and you get a nudge when you're close. The sooner you guess, the more you score, and the artist earns a bonus for every correct guess.

  4. 4

    Crown the champion

    Everyone takes a turn drawing each round. After three rounds, the highest score takes the trophy.
