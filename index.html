Draw it. Guess it. Win it.

Scribbly is a real-time multiplayer drawing and guessing game for Android. One player draws a secret word while everyone else races to guess it. The faster you guess, the more points you score.

Get it on Google Play

Free to play. Android 9 and newer.

Three ways to play

Private rooms

Create a room and share its code with up to 7 friends. Everyone joins from their own phone, wherever they are.

Play online

Pick a match size of 2, 3 or 4 players and Scribbly finds opponents for you. The game starts as soon as the room fills.

Play with the computer

No signal? Play a full game against a computer opponent, completely offline.

How a game works

  1. Each turn, one player picks one of three words and has 80 seconds to draw it.
  2. Everyone else types guesses. Letters of the word are revealed as the clock runs down, and you get a nudge when a guess is close.
  3. Correct guesses score more the sooner they come, and the artist earns a bonus for every player who gets it.
  4. Everyone takes a turn drawing each round. After three rounds, the highest score wins.

Words come from a bank of nearly 1,000 easy-to-draw words across 31 categories, from animals and food to space and mythology, and no word repeats within a game.

No account needed

Just type a name and play. Scribbly doesn't ask for an email, phone number or sign-in. Usernames, drawings and guesses are only kept while a game is running. Read the full privacy policy.
